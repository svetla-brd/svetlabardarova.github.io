Nuklai - Enterprise Website

Enterprise website for business leaders, data analysts, and IT professionals — streamlining complex data workflows with intuitive, AI-ready experiences.

The user personas guided the design of nuklai.io by ensuring that the website effectively addresses the distinct needs of various decision-makers. By focusing on their goals — such as optimizing AI data usage and maintaining data security — the website emphasizes ease of data integration, accessibility, and compliance.

Year
2024
Scope
Product Design, Branding

Target Audience & Problem Solved

Our primary audience includes business leaders, data analysts, and IT professionals working in mid-sized to large enterprises. They face the challenge of sifting through large datasets, struggling with inefficient data processing, and the complexity of adopting AI-driven technology. We help streamline these processes by offering intuitive, adaptable solutions that provide actionable insights without requiring specialized technical skills.

User Personas

The personas include key decision-makers like the CDO, CTO, and Business Intelligence Manager, all focused on leveraging AI and data to drive innovation, efficiency, and business growth. Their main challenges involve integrating diverse data, maintaining quality, and deriving actionable insights. They need user-friendly platforms, scalable solutions, and robust governance to meet their goals and ensure compliance.

Prototyping & User Testing

Our development process involved extensive prototyping, where user feedback played a critical role. Early-stage wireframes and interactive prototypes were continuously tested with our target audience. Insights gathered from usability tests helped us refine and enhance features, ensuring the final product was intuitive, effective, and addressed the users' pain points directly.

Wireframes

Detailed wireframes crafted after the Research phase, showcasing the User journey through the website.

Clear value proposition

We centered our messaging around our target audience and what they care about — How to activate their Data. How? Simply organize, enrich and let the analysis work for you. This separates us amongst our competitors.
